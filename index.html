Publish The Price And The Reasoning

Architecture practice · six people

Hidden fees turn the principal’s scarcest hours into unpaid qualification labour at the widest part of the funnel. Publish the structure and the logic behind it, move qualification upstream of the call, and make the thinking the exhibit.

Sell The Constraint

Single-product hardware · direct

When every page in a category looks identical, competition collapses to price — and a small manufacturer loses that war. So the product page becomes an argument: state plainly what the product refuses to do, above the buy button.
